<GuideLab id="M1-L1" title="Classify the media" objective="Determine whether each simulated drive contains Federal Contract Information.">

1. Read `MediaDisposalPolicy.pdf` and review the FCI definition.
2. Inspect `{{podPrefix}}-FCI-USB-Contents.txt`, including **Hidden Archive** and **Temp**. Note files tied to federal contract `FA-2026-{{podPrefix}}`.
3. Inspect `{{podPrefix}}-Employee-Handbook-Contents.txt`; it contains general internal documents but no contract data.
4. Cross-check media IDs in `MediaInventory.xlsx` and complete `MediaClassificationWorksheet.docx`.
5. In `MediaClassificationResponses.csv`, classify the FCI drive as `FCI` and the handbook drive as `Non-FCI`; cite specific filenames in each Evidence cell.

```text
{{podPrefix}}-FCI-USB.vhdx,FCI,Contracts and invoices reference federal contract FA-2026-{{podPrefix}}
{{podPrefix}}-Employee-Handbook.vhdx,Non-FCI,Only employee handbook benefits and holiday documents are present
```

**Success check:** both media rows contain the correct classification and meaningful evidence.

</GuideLab>

<GuideLab id="M1-L2" title="Sanitize media for reuse" objective="Create a new fully formatted volume and complete both sanitization records.">

1. Record the current `{{podPrefix}}-FCI-MEDIA` label and folders from the contents listing.
2. On your assigned server, open PowerShell as administrator.
3. Recreate the volume and perform a full NTFS format. Replace deleting files with the approved Clear process.

```powershell
$vhd = "{{mpArtifactsPath}}{{podPrefix}}-FCI-USB.vhdx"
Mount-DiskImage -ImagePath $vhd
$disk = Get-DiskImage -ImagePath $vhd | Get-Disk
Clear-Disk -Number $disk.Number -RemoveData -Confirm:$false
Initialize-Disk -Number $disk.Number -PartitionStyle MBR -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue
$part = New-Partition -DiskNumber $disk.Number -UseMaximumSize -AssignDriveLetter
Format-Volume -Partition $part -FileSystem NTFS -NewFileSystemLabel "{{podPrefix}}-SANITIZED" -Full -Confirm:$false
Get-ChildItem -Path "$($part.DriveLetter):\" -Force
Dismount-DiskImage -ImagePath $vhd
```

4. Confirm the label is exactly `{{podPrefix}}-SANITIZED` and the volume is empty apart from system folders.
5. Complete both `MediaSanitizationLog.csv` and `MediaSanitizationCertificate.csv` with MediaId `{{podPrefix}}-FCI-USB`, Method `Clear` or `Purge`, Result `Pass`, Disposition `Reuse`, your name, and a valid date.

**Success check:** the tracker finds a new empty NTFS volume and matching completed records.

</GuideLab>

<GuideLab id="M1-L3" title="Decide the disposition" objective="Use asset, custody, and vendor evidence to justify destruction of failed FCI media.">

1. Review `LaptopAssetRecord.csv`: `{{podPrefix}}-LAP-017` is end of life, contains FCI, failed diagnostics, and has serial `SN-{{podPrefix}}-88421`.
2. Confirm `ChainOfCustody.csv` sends that drive to an approved destruction vendor.
3. Confirm `VendorDestructionCertificate.txt` identifies certificate `VDC-{{podPrefix}}-2026-017`, the matching asset/serial, physical shredding, and status `DESTROYED`.
4. In `MP-M1-L3_DispositionWorksheet.csv`, record:

| Column        | Value                                                                                   |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AssetId       | `{{podPrefix}}-LAP-017`                                                                 |
| Decision      | `Destroy`                                                                               |
| DriveSerial   | `SN-{{podPrefix}}-88421`                                                                |
| CertificateId | `VDC-{{podPrefix}}-2026-017`                                                            |
| Rationale     | A full sentence citing failed diagnostics, FCI, end-of-life status, and the certificate |

Reuse is not appropriate because the drive failed diagnostics; returning it to inventory leaves FCI on unreliable end-of-life media.

<GuideSection id="quick-reference" index="QR" title="Quick reference">

| Term             | Meaning                                                         |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| FCI              | Government contract information not intended for public release |
| Clear            | Overwrite so standard recovery tools cannot retrieve data       |
| Purge            | Stronger sanitization that defeats laboratory recovery          |
| Destroy          | Physically render the media unusable                            |
| Chain of custody | Record of who held media, when, and why                         |

- Classify the entire drive by its most sensitive content.
- Read hidden-item listings before deciding a drive is non-FCI.
- Complete the sanitization log **and** certificate with the same values.
- Keep all answer CSV names and header rows unchanged.
- Use complete evidence and rationale, not one-word answers.

</GuideSection>
